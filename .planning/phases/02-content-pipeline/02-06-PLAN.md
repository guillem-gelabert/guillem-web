**One or more entries** — render one `<article>` per entry, in the order `publishedFor` returns

`next/link` to `postPath(locale, entry.slug)` with the entry title; then, nested for the gaps, a
`<p className="max-w-prose text-standfirst">` with the standfirst and a
`<PostMeta locale={locale} date={…} switchHref={null} draft={entry.frontmatter.draft} />`. Gap
tokens per the sketch: `gap-2xl` from the kicker to the first entry, `gap-lg` under the headline,
`gap-md` under the standfirst.

Three rules that make or break this, all from the UI-SPEC and PROJECT.md's Out of Scope list:

- **The headline is the only link inside an `<article>`.** No card, no border, no fill, no hover
  elevation, no "Read more". This is why `PostMeta` is passed `switchHref={null}` here: the
  always-present index switcher on the kicker line already satisfies "the index switcher is always
  rendered and points at the other locale's index", and a second link inside the article would put
  a competing affordance under a poster-scale headline — the exact failure D-10 exists to prevent.
  The per-piece language switch still appears on the post page itself, where a reader who has
  committed to the piece needs it. Record this reading in a comment.
- **`<h1>` is the small kicker and `<h2>` is the large headline.** This inversion is deliberate: a
  screen-reader user landing on the index needs to hear that this is the writing index, and a
  newspaper front page sets its own name small above its lead headline.
- **The entry title is the Display role and carries the trail** (Humane, through `SmearTitle`),
  unlike the post page where it is the Heading role. Display's job in this phase is this one
  poster-scale moment, and it is what makes the index read as deliberate at n=1.

**Two or more entries is a fallback, not a mode.** Repeat exactly the same `<article>` markup,
separated by an `<hr>`. No list layout, no row treatment, no dated index component, no second set
of styles, no new component. Do not cost a second render mode.

Export `generateMetadata` on both indexes returning a title, a description, `alternates.canonical`
set to `indexPath(locale)` and `alternates.languages` mapping `en` → `/writing`, `de` → `/texte`
and `x-default` → `/writing`.

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-02-25 | Tampering / Information disclosure | Path traversal through the German `[slug]` into `loadPostModule` | mitigate | Identical control to the English route: `findBySlug(await publishedFor("de"), slug)` then `notFound()`, both strictly before the dynamic import.
