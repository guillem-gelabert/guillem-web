<objective>
Ship the German edition. Phase 2's D-07 accepted this cost explicitly and deliberately: the writing
index is bilingual, so a case study that exists only in English leaves `/texte` at its empty state
for the whole of v1.

The risk is far lower than it looks, and research measured why. The author has a complete,
professional parallel German text of the underlying piece — *"Der Tourismus erwirtschaftet 45,5
Prozent der balearischen Wirtschaftsleistung"*, *"Die Hacke wich der Maurerkelle, das Feld der
Stadt"* — carrying the same argument sentence for sentence, including all six
`*Grafikbeschreibung:*` blocks that are the honest source for the German figure alt text. The
methodology page has an authoritative published German mirror, so beat 6 needs no translation
either. And this is a translation of locked English rather than independent composition.

The German title is settled and is not CONTEXT's provisional wording: the shipped German pivot
sentence is *"Dafür ändert sich die Darstellung."* — the subject is *Darstellung*, not *Grafik*,
and `Die Grafik ändert sich` appears nowhere in the author's German. Title
`Die Darstellung ändert sich`, slug `die-darstellung-aendert-sich`.

Purpose: CASE-01 and CASE-02 in the second locale; `/texte` stops being empty.
Output: one MDX file, the German twin of Plan 03's English.
</objective>
