fabricated claim and a live page under the author's name — the prose ships without a human
proofread, by directive, and D-19 is the compensating control. Research classified it as "not

quotation can be string-matched offline against the page it came from, and every number can be
traced to a named file. Worth doing properly, once, as a committed artifact.

this phase's actual deliverable. Green tests against a local build are not a published case study.

closes two conditions at once: Phase 2's rule that `/writing` at n=0 must never be the public

`HOME-01` tripwire, which STATE.md requires be re-asserted at the top of every subsequent phase's
carried-forward state until the user supplies the sentence. The site's single most important
sentence still reads `Developer.`
