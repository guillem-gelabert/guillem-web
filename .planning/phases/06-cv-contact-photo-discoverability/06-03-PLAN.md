<objective>
This is the one phase in the milestone that touches real personal facts, and this plan is where the
no-fabrication rule becomes mechanical rather than aspirational.

Five values must come from the user and must never be invented: the public contact email, the
LinkedIn profile URL, every row of employment history (plus education and languages), the
photograph file, and HOME-01's positioning sentence. A fabricated employment history or a wrong
email on a live job-hunting site is a serious, hard-to-detect failure; a labelled absence is not.

The pattern is already proven twice in this repo — `POSITIONING_PLACEHOLDER` in `lib/work.ts` and
`COPY_REVIEWED` in `lib/backlog.tsx` — and both records say the same thing: **mark it in source,
never on screen.** A live job-hunting site must never display `[goes here]`.

**This plan does not flip robots and no plan in this phase does.** `tests/unit/launch-gate.test.ts`
is the mechanism that makes that safe: it asserts `robots: { index: false }` in both root layouts
for exactly as long as any of the six blocking values is unfilled, and it runs in the fast tier on
every commit. It cannot be satisfied by editing one file.

Purpose: typed placeholders every downstream surface can render around, and one test that binds
them to the milestone's only irreversible act.
Output: `lib/cv.ts`, `lib/contact.ts`, and three `node --test` files.
</objective>
