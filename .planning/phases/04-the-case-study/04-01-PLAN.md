<objective>
Capture the phase's entire evidence base from the live published piece, in one wave, before
anything else depends on it.

Two outputs. The three figures — screenshots of real, settled states of
`ib-gdp.guillemgelabert.com` — are the only honest images this case study can carry: the
`ib-gdp-evolution` repository is private, `public/` holds nothing reusable, and every alternative
(redrawing the data, reconstructing the series, generating a chart) is fabrication. The live-text
snapshot is the offline authority for D-19's accuracy gate: research proved the vault drafts
differ from what shipped (`en_with_charts.md` says *"The data tell a more complex story"*; the
live page says *"The data tells a more complicated story."*), so every sentence this phase
presents as a quotation must be checkable against the live page and not against a working copy.

Research already drove the live site end to end and measured everything this plan needs — the
step→figure mapping (verified three independent ways), the SVG box (1200×820 CSS px), the settle
oracle, and the prose-card overlay hazard. This plan implements those measurements; it does not
re-derive them.

Purpose: discharge the one external, time-sensitive dependency in the phase. `04-RESEARCH.md`
states it plainly — the live piece is the only source for the figures, and a redeploy with a
changed Act 2 configuration would invalidate the mapping. Capture early.
Output: two one-shot scripts, three committed PNGs, four committed text snapshots.
</objective>
